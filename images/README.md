# Meal Photos

Drop a photo of a finished dish here and it automatically appears under that meal on the page.

**Naming:** dish name in lowercase, spaces/symbols replaced by hyphens, ending in `.jpg`.

Examples (this week's defaults):
- Sheet-Pan Kielbasa & Peppers  ->  sheet-pan-kielbasa-peppers.jpg
- Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry      ->  beef-broccoli-stir-fry.jpg
- Garlic Shrimp Tacos           ->  garlic-shrimp-tacos.jpg

Photos are matched by dish name, so they persist across weeks and reappear whenever that dish comes back.
